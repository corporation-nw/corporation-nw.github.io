Products & Services

Two technologies
to control light.

A coating that removes optical noise, and an ink that blocks unwanted light. We supply materials that support image quality in optical components, from smartphone cameras and beyond.

GLCGHOST LESS COATING

Technology that removes optical noise.

A low-reflection coating that suppresses reflections on lens surfaces and reduces the optical noise behind ghosting and flare.

  • Refractive-index-free design (Nd 1.15–1.60)
  • Industry-standard ghost suppression

CEO Message

Connecting technology and people,
toward a future no company
can reach alone.

Our mission at Shin World is to connect technology and people in the optics industry, and to keep creating new value together with customers around the world.

We believe that walls no single company can climb can always be overcome when those with technology work together. With that belief, we have grown our business across Asia — Japan, China and Taiwan first among them — built on sales and technical consulting in optics.

Drawing on years of expertise in optical design and optical materials, we connect Japanese companies with outstanding technology to Asian companies with manufacturing strength and speed. From distribution and equipment sales and start-up to supporting the development of new optical components, we stay with our customers from the first step to the last.

We will keep pursuing what optical technology can do, and keep taking on new challenges with our customers and partners, with our sights set on the world’s best.

President, Shin World Co., Ltd.Ryuji Kimura
